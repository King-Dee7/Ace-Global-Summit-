# ACE GLOBAL SUMMIT

## Summit Summary

## Why This Summit?

ACE GLOBAL SUMMIT is a global summit series built to connect capital, leadership, and opportunity across markets. It is not a fixed event tied to one country. It is designed to convene in different regions, edition by edition, wherever the greatest concentration of opportunity and audience can be brought together.

The inaugural edition will be hosted in Ghana this December. December is peak season for diaspora return and international travel into Ghana, delivering a substantial global audience already in-market. This makes Ghana the natural launch point for a summit series built, from its first edition, to operate on a global stage.

The summit's core mandate is to connect capital with opportunity. Each edition brings together global leaders, investors, and innovators to identify where the next decade of growth is being built, and gives attendees direct access to the people building it through expert panels, keynote addresses, fireside conversations, and specially-curated Investor engagement and networking sessions spanning Africa, Europe, the Americas, and Asia, and Australia.

Every edition of ACE GLOBAL SUMMIT operates on the same principle: proximity. Proximity to capital, to decision-makers, to the strategies used by today's leading operators, and to the sectors positioned to define the next global growth cycle. Attendees leave with actionable insight, working networks, and, in many cases, active deals in motion.

## Focus Areas

Each edition of ACE GLOBAL SUMMIT is organized around four core pillars:

## 1. Wealth Creation, Entrepreneurship & Investment

Capital formation, venture funding, business scaling, and the strategies driving long-term wealth creation across markets.

## 2. Culture & Creative Economy

The commercial infrastructure behind entertainment, media, fashion, and the creative industries, and their growing contribution to global GDP.

## 3. Hospitality & Logistics

Tourism and hospitality infrastructure, alongside the supply chains and logistics networks that move goods, services, and people across borders.

## 4. Emerging Technology

The technologies reshaping global industry — fintech, AI, and the infrastructure investment required to compete in the next decade.

## Who This Is For

This is a high-level convening of global leaders, business magnates, investors, venture capitalists, fund managers, C-suite executives, policymakers, and entrepreneurs across sectors. Every session is built to deliver comprehensive, practical insight into business, investment, finance, and market trends — insight attendees can act on immediately.

## Summit Structure

The summit is currently planned across two days.

## Day One — Main Conference

Keynote addresses, panels, and fireside chats spanning the four focus areas, delivered by leaders and practitioners from Africa, Europe, the Americas, and Asia.

## Day Two — Investors Roundtable & Dealroom

A closed, curated session connecting investors, venture capitalists, and fund managers directly with vetted founders and ventures across the four focus areas. Built for deal-making, not discussion.

## What Success Looks Like

ACE GLOBAL SUMMIT succeeds when capital finds ventures worth funding, when cross-border partnerships are formed and followed through, and when attendees leave with a clear, actionable plan for the year ahead — not simply new contacts.

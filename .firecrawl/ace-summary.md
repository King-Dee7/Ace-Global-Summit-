The ACE Global Summit is a dynamic series aimed at connecting capital, leadership, and opportunities across various markets globally. The inaugural event is set for December in Ghana, coinciding with peak international travel and diaspora engagement. The summit focuses on four main areas: Wealth Creation, Culture & Creative Economy, Hospitality & Logistics, and Emerging Technology, attracting global leaders, investors, and innovators. The structure includes a two-day format with keynotes and discussions on the first day and a dedicated investors roundtable on the second day, fostering actionable insights and partnerships for attendees.

The ACE Global Summit, taking place on December 23-24, 2026, at the Accra Marriott Hotel in Ghana, is a premier forum aimed at connecting global leaders, investors, and innovators to foster investment and cultural exchange across Africa and the global Black diaspora. The summit is designed to catalyze trade and economic synergy by positioning Ghana as a strategic gateway for investment.

Key objectives include facilitating connections between capital and credible African ventures, establishing Accra as a recurring destination for global investment, forging partnerships, and translating engagement into actionable outcomes.

The summit will focus on four main areas: wealth creation, hospitality and logistics, culture and the creative economy, and emerging technology. It includes a structured program with keynote addresses, investor engagement sessions, and networking opportunities.

Strategic partnership opportunities are available, including title sponsorships and partnerships in specific focus areas.

Overall, the ACE Global Summit aims to harness the peak return of diasporic visitors in December to boost Ghana's global economic presence.
